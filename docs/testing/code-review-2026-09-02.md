Manual review concentrated on financial mutations and their callers, collection/provider state transitions, authorization and retries, background delivery, reports, monetary inputs, contracts and operational scripts. ## Intentional functional restrictions

- Supplier replacement of an accepted repayment schedule returns `409 accepted_schedule_locked`. A buyer-accepted amendment workflow is not implemented.
- Corrections reaching NGN 10,000 (1,000,000 kobo) cumulatively per obligation are blocked until a verified independent approval workflow exists. Naming another person is not approval.
- Mono production activation remains rejected. Sandbox credentials and Sweep access were unavailable. Reconciliation requires the original configured sandbox credentials.
- Uncertain bank outcomes retain the debit reservation. Manual payment, write-off or dispute adjustment cannot consume that reserved amount; reconcile first.

## Remaining launch evidence and review limits

The code gaps in the original review were addressed in the [completion record](code-completion-2026-09-02.md), including report snapshots, financial read errors, transactional notices, prior-debit delivery gating, reconciliation cases and durable metrics. The restrictions above remain intentional.

1. **Provider proof:** Complete the 21 scenarios in [Mono acceptance evidence](mono-sweep-evidence.md), including hosted buyer consent, aggregate partial recovery, timeout lookup, cancellation, reversal and actual settlement. Local HTTPS mocks do not certify Mono.
2. **Notices and operations:** Prove agreed notice timing, successful delivery and failure escalation before enabling automatic debit. The follow-up enforces confirmed prior-notice delivery and waiting time, persists financial lifecycle notices with their originating transaction, and exports durable financial metrics. Actual connector certification and alert-delivery drills remain outstanding.
3. **Financial policy:** Verify provider setup/account/debit costs against the supplier pricing and negotiated contract, taxation, settlement ownership, refunds and credits. A balanced internal journal alone does not prove funds reached the supplier. No undisclosed buyer fee was introduced. Mono documents provider charges separately in its [Direct Debit overview](https://docs.mono.co/docs/payments/direct-debit/overview).
4. **Regulatory application:** Confirm the actual operating model, partner responsibilities, licensing classification and complaint/dispute process with qualified Nigerian counsel/compliance reviewers. “Supplier-funded trade credit” is a product description, not a legal exemption. The [CBN payments supervision page](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/PaymentsSystem/index.html) identifies the revised direct-debit framework; the exact current obligations need the applicable instrument and provider contract, not inference from code.
5. **Privacy and records:** Approve the purpose/lawful-basis and retention register, processor/transfer arrangements, deletion versus financial evidence holds, DPIA where applicable, and human correction/appeal paths for repayment history. Existing inventory entries explicitly retain pending legal-approval markers. The [NDPC Act listing](https://ndpc.gov.ng/download/nigeria-data-protection-act-2023) was located; its full document fetch returned 403 during this review, so no claim of clause-by-clause legal verification is made.
6. **Coverage:** Automated package/type/syntax checks do not establish behavioural correctness of every unchanged file. The follow-up fixes the financial list paths and exercises browser journeys with mocked APIs. Unchanged legacy utilities are not given blanket behavioural assurance. Neither those reports nor the coverage inventory should be treated as a blanket clean bill of health.
