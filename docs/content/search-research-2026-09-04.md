# Kredit article research — 4 September 2026

Research and editorial planning only. No articles were written or published in this pass. The separate design change replaces the article page’s “Continue with useful pages” section with a responsive, four-link navigation section headed “Find what you need next.”

## Recommendation

Build a substantial library about selling goods on credit and getting the records and payments right. Start with practical guides, worked Nigerian-naira examples and usable templates. Expand into industry guides only when interviews or credible operational evidence give them a distinct purpose.

The problem with the previous large library was repetitive, thin treatment—not the ambition to publish 100 articles. Long-form content is appropriate where it answers the reader's whole problem. A fixed article count or word-count quota is not a quality standard.

The accompanying [100-topic research map](article-research-2026-09-04.csv) contains **66 standalone candidates, 25 topics requiring additional validation, and nine topics to merge into other guides**. These are not 100 proven opportunities or approved articles. P1/P2/P3 describe editorial sequencing, not search volume or ranking difficulty. There are 34 P1, 40 P2 and 26 P3 entries; legal review can hold a P1 topic back.

## Evidence and limitations

This research sampled thematic web searches, Nigerian provider help centres and content libraries, official services, and Google documentation. It establishes recurring problems and existing topic coverage. It does **not** measure how many Nigerians search each phrase. No Keyword Planner export, Search Console account, paid keyword database, customer interviews or comprehensive individual SERP review was available. Related query variants in the map are explicitly hypotheses. Source leads attached to a cluster are starting points, not proof of every claim in every proposed article.

## Long-form editorial brief standard

Use these as planning ranges, not minimums: roughly **1,500–2,500 words for a substantial operational guide**, **2,500–4,000 for a broad pillar**, and **800–1,600 plus a working asset for a template-led page**. Expand or shorten according to the job. Google explicitly says it has no preferred word count. Originality, completeness and useful firsthand evidence matter more than padding. [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).

Each commissioned brief should specify:

1. Reader and situation: who is searching, what just happened, and what they need to accomplish.
2. Primary intent and adjacent questions: one clear page purpose, plus exclusions and related pages.
3. A direct answer, then a complete process with decisions, exceptions and a checked example.
4. Evidence for factual claims, actual authorship, and a qualified reviewer where needed.
5. At least one substantial explanatory visual or functional download; usually two to four for a broad guide when useful.
6. A natural next step. Mention Kredit only where its verified current behaviour helps that task.

Avoid interchangeable introductions, inflated promises, invented merchants or quotes, unsupported statistics, formulaic conclusions, artificial local references and repeated industry pages with nouns swapped. A synthetic example must say it is illustrative. An actual merchant story needs consent and attribution. Do not imply independent comparison when Kredit is the publisher.

## Visual research direction

Prefer explanatory assets: annotated invoices and receipts; sale-to-payment timelines; running-balance tables; decision trees; editable trackers; actual product screenshots; original photographed or documented merchant workflows where permission exists. Decorative stock illustrations do not substitute for explanation.

Use one internally consistent example across an article: names, quantities, dates, payments and balances must reconcile. Label illustrative data. Never turn invented market figures into a chart. Downloads need realistic test cases including partial payments, overpayments, returns and corrections where relevant.

For publication, provide captions, useful alt text, readable labels, responsive images, semantic data tables and mobile-friendly downloads. Place images near the relevant explanation and expose them through normal image elements. [Google's image guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images) supports contextual images and descriptive alternatives; keyword stuffing is counterproductive.

## SEO publication requirements

“Fully SEO compliant” should mean technically sound, useful and eligible for discovery—not a ranking guarantee.

- Assign one canonical URL per distinct intent. Preserve relevant existing slugs, consolidate overlaps and link between genuinely related guides. Avoid near-identical city or industry pages created to capture variations. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
- Use a clear, accurate title, a descriptive summary, one principal heading and useful subsection headings. Answer the question without delaying it for a long introduction. Google can generate a different snippet from page content; rigid character counts are not a guarantee. [Snippet documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).
- Give each page crawlable internal links with descriptive text and a clear place in its topic hub. Check canonical, indexability, status code, sitemap entry and redirects when a URL changes. [Crawlable link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).
- Match Article structured data to visible facts: headline, representative image, real author and genuine publication/update dates. Add an author page where appropriate; never invent credentials. [Article documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article).
- **Do not build the strategy around FAQ rich results.** Google's changelog says FAQ rich results stopped appearing on 7 May 2026, with documentation removal noted in June. Questions can still be useful editorial content. [Google Search updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).
- Optimize mobile readability, image weight and loading behaviour. Measure performance when real traffic exists; good Core Web Vitals do not guarantee rankings. [Core Web Vitals guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals).

## Specialist and industry gates

Agreement templates, recovery letters, court procedures, record retention, privacy duties and bad-debt treatment require qualified review before publication. The map marks these as validation work. Research sources include [Lagos Judiciary's 2023 practice direction](https://lagoscomis.lagosjudiciary.gov.ng/assets/files/small_claims_pd.pdf), [FCCPC FAQs](https://fccpc.gov.ng/about-us/faqs/) and NDPC. Verify amendments and applicability: digital-lending rules are not automatically rules for every business-to-business credit sale. Old CBN documents can appear in recent search results; crawl dates do not establish current law. No universal cancellation period, court limit or retention period is approved by this report.

All ten industry candidates remain conditional. FMCG has contextual coverage, including [BusinessDay's report about credit sales](https://businessday.ng/market-intelligence/article/weakening-spending-power-forces-fmcg-firms-to-sell-on-credit/), but this is not proof of search demand for all sectors. Interview operators about distinctive documents, units, returns, delivery evidence and payment cycles. If a sector adds no meaningful difference, cover it as an example inside a broader guide.

## Validation before writing

1. Export Nigeria-targeted keyword ideas and historical metrics, recording date range, language and network. Group close variants before assessing opportunities. Keyword Planner averages depend on settings and include close variants; its competition metric concerns advertisers, not organic ranking difficulty. [Google Ads metric definitions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en).
2. Inspect the actual results for each proposed standalone query. Record dominant intent, formats, Nigerian relevance, recurring questions and the gap Kredit can substantiate. Do not report autocomplete or People Also Ask evidence unless observed.
3. Interview suppliers and buyers, starting with a small proposed sample of five suppliers across relevant sectors. This has not happened yet. Ask for anonymized recurring questions and permission to use examples.
4. Reclassify the 100 rows: retain, merge, defer or reject. Low measured volume alone need not eliminate a high-value customer-help page; be explicit about that purpose.
5. Build and check assets, obtain factual reviews, then write in batches. After launch, review query/page/country impressions, clicks and click-through rate in Search Console, alongside actual reader usefulness. Query reports omit some low-volume or anonymized data. [Search Console reporting limitations](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17011259?hl=en).

The next writing decision should follow this validation, not an automatic instruction to produce 100 pages.
