# Dispute adjustment

Owner: disputes operations. Confirm the dispute decision, evidence timeline,
blocked amount, and approval threshold. Collection remains blocked only for the
contested amount unless the decision says otherwise.

Post an immutable, balanced adjustment and record the decision actor and
reason. High-value write-offs and fee waivers require the configured second
approver. If the decision changes, append a new decision and compensating
adjustment; never rewrite history.
