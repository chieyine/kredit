# Buyer and seller usability sessions

Status: prepared, not conducted. Real buyers, sellers and operators must participate before usability is claimed. Use an isolated demonstration environment and fictional data; do not ask participants to send money or authorize a real bank account.

Recruit people who reflect the intended users, including someone with a small-screen phone, limited connectivity and someone who relies on a screen reader or keyboard. Obtain consent for observations; record task outcomes without copying sensitive personal or bank information. Assign a facilitator and note taker.

Ask participants to explain what they believe happened before giving help. Record independent completion, assistance needed, hesitation, mistaken interpretation, recovery behavior and accessibility barriers. Sample size and acceptance criteria belong to the product owner; a small session does not establish population-wide usability.

| Scenario | Prompt | Essential understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create a sale | Use the chosen business's normal terms, then change the first due date for this customer. | The correct business, amount, due date and extra hours are visible; saving a draft does not send an agreement or move money. |
| Accept and authorize | Review an offer and explain what accepting it and granting bank permission each do. | Acceptance and bank permission are distinct; collection is conditional and not guaranteed. |
| Unconfirmed action | After a simulated interrupted response, explain the next step. | Keep the same details; retry the same action or check the record. Do not send another transfer or recreate the sale. Locate the support reference. |
| Delivery problem | Report missing goods, then describe what happens when the seller corrects delivery or accepts a return. | Buyer confirms actual corrected receipt; seller accepts an actual return. Neither party should falsely confirm goods merely to clear a warning. |
| Payment report | Report a transfer made yesterday and inspect the sale balance. | Enter the real Nigerian payment time. A buyer report is not confirmed payment; the seller verifies evidence before the balance changes. |
| Operator handover | Take an unassigned financial review, write what was checked, then hand it over. | Ownership and notes change; balances and discrepancy status do not. Closure requires the underlying records to agree. |
| Seller payout | Explain a successful customer debit with an outstanding seller settlement. | Customer collection and seller payout are separate; recording bank evidence does not send money or pay the customer debt twice. |

After each session, ask: “What do you believe is owed now?”, “Who needs to act next?”, and “What would you do if the connection disappeared here?” Treat an incorrect money/authorization belief as a release concern even when the participant completed the clicks.

Log each observation with screen, task, device/accessibility mode, user interpretation, expected interpretation, severity, proposed change and follow-up outcome. Keep observations separate from the facilitator's assumptions. No session results or participant approvals have been fabricated for this guide.
