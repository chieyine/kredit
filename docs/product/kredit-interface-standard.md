# Kredit interface standard

Kredit records supplier-funded business trade. It does not lend money or guarantee
repayment. Product words and visuals must describe the authoritative state, not the
state a successful request usually implies.

## Working screens

Primary navigation is Home, Sales, Customers and Payments. Add sale is a visible
action. Limits, reporting, team, messages, settings and support remain reachable.
Working screens use compact sans-serif headings, exact tabular money, readable
labels and large controls. The public site retains its paper-and-ink editorial
character. Motion may clarify an interaction; it must never animate financial
figures through incorrect values or obscure the next action. Reduced-motion,
low-data and forced-colour settings remain supported.

## Words and states

Use “Draft sale”, not “You never finished this sale”. Use “Transfer reported”, not
“Payment received”, until receipt is confirmed. State the party, amount and next
action. Distinguish agreement accepted, bank permission pending, ready to release,
goods released, goods received and payment confirmed. Never turn missing data into
zero or an empty state. No fake testimonials, customer counts or guaranteed recovery.

A dispute may hold only its contested amount or all new debits, depending on the
recorded effect. A request already sent to a bank may still complete. All public and
private explanations must preserve that distinction.

## Nigerian business use

Accept local 11-digit and +234 phone formats. Use NGN/kobo and Africa/Lagos dates.
Show a familiar trading name without concealing the contracting party. Support
clear goods quantities, partial payments, reported transfers and delivery problems.
Provide help when a transfer reference is unavailable; do not invent a reference or
recognise money from a screenshot. Provider authorisation is a separate, explained
step with a verified destination. Shared-device drafts must be optional, scoped and
short-lived. Test these choices with actual users before claiming comprehension.

## Release acceptance

The critical sale, buyer consent, delivery, transfer-report and dispute journeys
must pass positive, rejected, unavailable, timeout, duplicate and out-of-order cases.
Accessibility automation is a gate, not a substitute for real-device testing.
Financial reconciliation after concurrency and restore tests must remain exact.
External release approval cannot be inferred from build success.
